Four Milestones · One Evolving Idea
Thirty-six saved early HTML files represent three distinct pre-repository designs; the fourth milestone is the original final experience.
v1"AI Fluency & Organizational Readiness"early one-pager
A static, single-column bio timeline — cream paper, serif type (Playfair / DM Sans), a red accent, and a "More Sections To Come" placeholder. Created for a specific one-to-one conversation, not a general audience. No phases, no interactivity, no teal. A different artifact wearing the same ambition.
v2"The Progression"warm palette · first hub
The first interactive version: a real phase model, eight phases, a "Work Tie-Ins" block, and the thesis already present in Jarvis-Ceiling form. Still on the old warm/cream palette. The concept flipped here — from a one-pager to a navigable hub.
v14"The Progression"teal tracker · repo start
The unified teal system, the segmented pill tracker, and a deliberately empty "Open Slot" phase. This is the exact file that opened Session 2 and became commit #1. Everything after this point is captured in git.
Final"The AI Arc"shipped
Six phases, the thesis moved to the end and rewritten, an eight-tile personal-app gallery with color-themed modals, and a Phase 06 built on its own template. About 5,415 words of original content, from a 270-word start.
Five Decisions That Shaped the Experience
The point isn't that the design changed. It's why each choice better supported the story the experience needed to tell.
01 · Audience & structure archived snapshots + retrospective notes

Beyond the one-pager

Changed: A static document prepared for one conversation became a phase-based interactive journey.

Why: The broader lesson was an unfolding progression, not a résumé of milestones. Readers needed room to explore the connections between phases.

02 · Interaction design reconstructed from working transcript

Two navigators rejected

Tried: A puzzle-piece grid and an accordion-like segmented bar.

Why they lost: The puzzle implied interchangeable parts rather than progression. The accordion made inactive phases hard to read. The segmented tracker with a separate summary kept the entire journey visible.

03 · Learning frame retrospective design notes

Personal story as evidence

Changed: The narrative shifted from recounting what happened to identifying lessons others could apply.

Why: Personal experimentation mattered because it illustrated relevance, small wins, and growing confidence—not because the page needed to be a biography.

04 · Information architecture v2/v14 snapshots + notes

Make reflection legible

Changed: A summary panel gave the selected phase a stable home. Bento layouts paired the account of what happened with what was learned; redundant “Work Tie-Ins” were cut.

Why: Interaction and visual variety should support comprehension, not force the visitor to decode a busy interface.

05 · Final synthesis later tracked revisions

From eight phases to six—and a thesis earned at the end

Changed: The phase structure was consolidated, the application gallery gained its own presentation, and the concluding idea moved to the final phase.

Why: The conclusion works better after the visitor has seen curiosity turn into practical experimentation, expanding capability, and then an interest in helping others learn.

New Wording, Same DNA
No matching eight-word phrases were found between v1 and the original finished page. What carried forward was the thinking, not the wording.
  • v1: "The certification was one stop. This is the larger arc it belongs to."  became the entire framing — and the name "The AI Arc."
  • v1: a personal college-football simulation  became the Phase 03 entry story and its personal-first takeaway.
  • v1: genuine UMD course completion  became Phase 02 and the reason the whole document exists.
  • v1: early personal tools including QuickSum and LiftBuddy  became the foundation for an eight-application gallery, with additional builds added as experimentation continued.
  • v1: a meeting-specific one-pager  became a general-audience case study — the memoir→evidence reframe started as an audience pivot.

The whole distance, in one line

The finished experience shares its subject and underlying evidence with the first draft, but almost none of its original phrasing. From a 270-word static page to a much richer interactive journey, the work changed because the thinking changed. The design history makes that progression visible.
